One row per team: the analysts at their desks and the manager (the trader) at the end of the row. Couriers carry data from the sources at the dock to the analysts, setups to the managers, orders and reports to the COO, and the COO's reports to your desk at the far end, which shows green while you are on the dashboard. Strategy lab · the validation gate —

Every strategy version moves through one pipeline: proposed, built on paper, tested, then reviewed against seven checks on its paper record, oldest trade first: enough trades, profit after charges, profit with double the charges, the latest 30% of trades still profitable (out of sample), target hits better than chance, profitable on most days, and a losing streak in limits. Only APPROVED strategies may reach the execution gateway. Passing is a prerequisite, not proof of future profit.

Today's forecast —

The forecast is built at 08:45 IST on trading days.

How to read this: the score is a weighted sum of six public, checkable inputs (world tone, the index's own trend, yesterday's close within its range, option positioning, what foreign institutions hold in index futures, and India VIX), plus the opening gap after 09:16. The range is one standard deviation of a day from India VIX: the market itself expects about two days in three to stay inside it. The weights are our own and unproven; every evening the forecast is scored against what happened and the record is shown above. It organises evidence; it does not predict.

Every market feed (Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Mint, Business Standard, BusinessLine, NDTV Profit, CNBC-TV18, Google News) and NSE's filings are read about once a minute in market hours. Each headline that names one listed company is scored for emotion: Euphoria and Optimism are positive, Fear and Panic negative. It goes straight to the intraday desk's Headlines or Filings hunter. A signal is not a trade: the desk enters only when the price moves the same way near its day high or low, with a stop beyond VWAP and Rs 150 of risk. Every hour your Claude analyst confirms or doubts the strongest signals; a doubted company is not traded for the rest of the day.

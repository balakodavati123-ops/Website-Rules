Real money tickets for you to place, and how to connect a broker

Connecting a broker

This page cannot send orders. Orders can only go through a broker that offers a Claude connector with order tools, added by you in Claude under Settings, Connectors. Zerodha publishes one for Kite (add a custom connector with the address mcp.kite.trade/mcp and sign in with your Kite account). INDmoney's connector, which this page already uses, is read-only.

Once a broker is connected, tell Claude in the chat. Claude will check which tools the broker really allows, and then place an order only when you approve that order. Fully automatic orders with no approval are not set up here.

How this desk works, and what it cannot do

Live figures come from your INDmoney account each time you open the page. Agent notes, the paper ledger, the pre-market plan and crypto prices (CoinDCX, checked against Kraken) are saved by the desk each weekday morning. The Live floor runs fixed rules on those live prices; it is a screen, not a forecast. The page cannot place or approve orders. Paper trades use pretend money; the desk moves to real money only after 100 or more paper trades and a 98% chance, after correction for the strategies tried, that the average trade makes money. A 98% confidence is not a 98% win rate. Nothing here is investment advice, and trading can lose money you cannot spare.

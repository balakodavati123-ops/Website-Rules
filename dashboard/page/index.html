How this desk works, and what it cannot do

Live figures come from your INDmoney account and Gemini each time you open the page. Agent notes, the paper ledger and the pre-market plan are saved by the desk after each run. The page cannot place or approve orders. Paper trades use pretend money; the desk moves to real money only after 100 or more paper trades and a 98% chance, after correction for the strategies tried, that the average trade makes money. A 98% confidence is not a 98% win rate. Nothing here is investment advice, and trading can lose money you cannot spare.
